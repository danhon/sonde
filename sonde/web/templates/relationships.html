Relationships

who I actually talk to rank by interaction type →

A different question from influence. Influence asks whether someone matters; this asks whether we know each other — so a 700k-follower columnist I have never spoken to ranks low, and a friend with 400 followers ranks high. Weighted by what an interaction costs to give: a conversation counts for a lot, a like for almost nothing, and interactions in both directions count for more than a stream in one.

Silence is not always absence, so up to 30 of the 100 points come from attention scarcity: someone with 150,000 followers who follows only a thousand accounts, one of them mine, has made a deliberate and narrow choice. That registers even with nothing said. It is capped low enough that it can lift a quiet follower into view but never outrank an actual conversation.
